Substitute Paraeducator📚

My name is Garrett Elijah Cohen and I am currently a substitute paraeducator that works for the Corona Norco Unified School District. My primary goal is to become an elementary school teacher, but as I continue my education in order to obtain a bachelor’s degree and teaching credentials, I have been working at multiple different school sites as a sub-aide of sorts.

I have worked plenty with students of all elementary school grade levels, from some experience with preschool and kindergarten, all the way up to sixth grade. However, if given the choice, I would prefer most to work with third grade, fourth grade, and fifth grade. Nevertheless, if the only possible position were to be with first grade, second grade, or sixth grade, then I can remain flexible and work with them too; it is just a preference after all. The only real potential barrier is working with a special education class. Now, through my university classes, I do have some experience with what to expect of students from many different types of disabilities. However, I would say that entering and working with a special education class is definite difficulty I come across. That being said, if a student with special needs were to be placed with a general education class with some additional supports in place, then that would be of little issue for me. I can handle it, I assure you!

At many different school sites within the Corona Norco Unified School District have I gained some valuable experience with working with children. The main schools I have frequented are Garretson Elementary, Jefferson Elementary, Adams Elementary, Susan B. Anthony Elementary, and Home Gardens Academy. However, I should stress that at many of these school sites, most of my time at them was within their different after school programs, filling in an absence. Whether they did ASES, ELOP, Excel, or a different service, I would argue that after school programs would be my bread and better for lack of as better term. I would say that these experiences are quite valuable altogether though, as the grade level I worked with at these school sites varies greatly from day to day. One day I fill in for a teacher who mainly covers first grade, and the next I do similar with another school doing sixth grade instead. Even though most of my current career was spent within different after school programs, I still have been able to take the odd assignment in a general education day class in the morning. While rarer, and typically with kindergarten students, I do have a solid foundation on what to expect out of a classroom and its typical day to day. To conclude, while my career has varied greatly over the past two years, the knowledge and experiences I have gained from it are irreplaceable. Overall, I would describe myself as pretty flexible, and quite a worthy choice to have at your school if you are willing to have me. I am always willing to learn, and will strive to do my very best!
